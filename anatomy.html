LAYER 01 • ACOUSTIC RESONATOR

01. Keycaps & Acoustic Profiles

The primary tactile touchpoint and hollow resonance chamber of every keystroke.

PBT / ABS 1.5mm - 1.7mm Wall Thickness Cherry / OEM / MOA / SA
Material Physics: PBT vs ABS

The chemical density and molding process of keycaps directly dictate acoustic pitch, bottom-out feedback, and resistance to finger oils over years of use.

  • •
    PBT (Polybutylene Terephthalate): Higher material density, chalky matte texture, resistant to shine. Produces a significantly deeper, lower-pitch sound frequency (The "Deep Thock").
  • •
    ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene): High-impact rigid thermoplastic. Exceptional double-shot legend sharpness and crisp high-frequency "clack". Develops an authentic patina shine over time.
Profile Geometry & Chamber Volume

The internal air volume trapped beneath the keycap acts as an acoustic soundbox, tuning the mechanical bottom-out snap.

  • •
    Cherry Profile: Ergonomic sculpted rows (R1-R4), medium height. Minimal resonance chamber, delivering balanced, clean keystrokes without hollow reverb.
  • •
    SA / KAT / MT3: High-profile spherical keycaps with cavernous internal chambers. Amplifies low-end bass resonance, creating a loud, deep, booming thock.
  • •
    MOA / XDA: Uniform height and rounded top curvature. Delivers dense, uniform marbly clacks across the entire keyboard plane.
ThocKeebs Tuning Standard

We mandate minimum 1.5mm wall thickness on all custom builds. Thin 1.0mm keycaps resonate with a hollow, plastic ping regardless of how well the switches are lubed.

LAYER 02 • MECHANICAL CORE

02. Mechanical Switches & 5-Part Anatomy

The mechanical heartbeat where keystrokes are actuated, dampened, and acoustically defined.

Linear / Tactile / Clicky Krytox GPL 205g0 + GPL 105 Long Pole / Standard
Exploded Switch Breakdown
  • •
    Top Housing: Polycarbonate (crisp, high pitch) or Nylon PA66 (deep, muted). Guides the stem sliders during actuation and shapes the top-out return sound.
  • •
    Stem & Sliders: Engineered from POM, LY, UPE, or P3 polymers for low-friction self-lubricating glide. Long-pole stems bottom out early (3.2mm - 3.5mm) on the center pole, creating an explosive popping thock.
  • •
    Precision Spring: Single-stage, two-stage extended (18-22mm), or slow-curve high-tensile steel. Determines actuation force (37g - 67g) and bounceback speed.
  • •
    Contact Leaf: Gold-plated phosphor bronze alloy. Closes the circuit when pushed by the stem leg. Requires precise lubing to prevent leaf ping without killing tactile bump.
  • •
    Bottom Housing: Features the center stem well and guide rails. The impact zone of the stem pole that generates the foundational keystroke sound.
ThocKeebs Precision Lubing Protocol

Factory switches suffer from scratchy slider friction, spring crunch, and metallic leaf ping. Our meticulous hand-tuning restores buttery perfection.

  • •
    Stem Rails: Micro-brushed with an exact 0.05mm layer of genuine Krytox GPL 205g0 to eliminate rail friction without feeling sluggish.
  • •
    Springs Bag-Lubed: Immersed and evenly coated in Krytox GPL 105 high-viscosity oil to eradicate 100% of spring crunch and high-frequency metallic ping.
  • •
    Switch Films: Optional 0.15mm Poron or HTV/PC films installed between housings to eliminate micro-wobble and deepen sound pitch.
Acoustic Secret

Long pole stems (e.g., Leobog Graywood, KTT Strawberry, BCP, Vertex V1) hit the bottom housing exclusively on the stem tip, focusing 100% of kinetic energy into a deep, solid bottom-out pop.

LAYER 03 • STABILIZATION ENGINE

03. Stabilizers & Anti-Rattle Modding

Ensuring multi-unit modifier keys (Spacebar, Enter, Shift, Backspace) actuate uniformly without wire ticking.

PCB Screw-in (Durock / Staebies) Wire Balancing Holee Mod + XHT-BDZ
Stabilizer Architecture

A single wobbly spacebar can ruin an otherwise pristine keyboard build. Stabilizers utilize a stainless steel torsion wire held by two slider housings to keep long keycaps level.

  • •
    PCB Screw-In Mount: Bolted directly to the PCB with steel screws and non-conductive washers. Immune to displacement during keycap pulling and vastly superior to clip-in stabs.
  • •
    Tolerance Engineering: Premium stabs (TX AP, Staebies V2.1) feature sub-0.05mm stem tolerances to eliminate lateral keycap wobble.
ThocKeebs 4-Step Anti-Rattle Tuning
  • •
    01. Wire Balancing: Every steel wire is tested on a precision ceramic flat surface with high-intensity backlighting. Bent ends are micro-adjusted by hand until 0.00mm gap remains.
  • •
    02. Stem Clipping: Eliminating superfluous dampening legs on older housings to provide a flat, crisp bottom-out landing.
  • •
    03. The Holee Mod: Medical micropore adhesive tape or PTFE strips applied inside the slider cavity. Cushions the steel wire impact and permanently silences ticking.
  • •
    04. Krytox XHT-BDZ Lube: Ultra-high viscosity fluorinated grease applied on wire elbows to dampen micro-chatter without creating a mushy return.
Guaranteed Result

Zero rattle, zero wire ticking on left/right edges of your Spacebar, with instantaneous snappy return and a deep, thocky acoustic strike.

LAYER 04 • ACOUSTIC FILTER

04. Switch Plate & Material Physics

The structural plate holds all switches securely in place, directly tuning bottom-out hardness and sound pitch.

PC / FR4 / POM / Aluminum / Brass Flex Index: 1 - 5 Acoustic Pitch Filter
Plate Materials Compared
  • •
    Polycarbonate (PC): Very flexible, soft typing feel. Absorbs harsh high frequencies to deliver a deep, creamy, thocky acoustic profile. The gold standard for thock lovers.
  • •
    FR4 (Epoxy Glass Cloth): The same composite used in PCBs. Balanced flexibility with a clean, poppy, marbly sound signature. Highly recommended all-rounder.
  • •
    POM (Polyoxymethylene): Flexible with a distinct bouncy bottom-out. Produces a uniquely muted, bass-heavy acoustic thud.
  • •
    Aluminum: Rigid, classic mechanical keyboard feel. Crisp high-pitch "clack" with immediate tactile bottom-out confirmation.
  • •
    Brass: Extremely stiff and heavy. Bright, loud acoustic clack with zero plate flex. Delivers maximum finger energy feedback.
Flex Cuts & Perimeter Relief

Modern custom plates incorporate laser relief cuts between switch clusters and perimeter relief tabs. This allows the plate assembly to compress downward uniformly under finger pressure, dampening wrist strain during extended typing or gaming sessions.

  • •
    Full-Plate: Single continuous sheet. Consistent sound and uniform bottom-out across all alpha keys.
  • •
    Half-Plate: Alphas have no plate (switches solder directly to PCB) for supreme bounce, while modifiers remain supported by plate tabs.
Plate Formula

Want deepest bass thock? Pair a Polycarbonate (PC) or POM plate with nylon switch housings and thick PBT keycaps.

LAYER 05 • SUSPENSION & REBOUND

05. Mounting Architecture

How the plate and PCB assembly is suspended inside the case dictates overall typing bounce and vibration transfer.

Gasket Mount Gummy O-Ring Top Mount Tray Mount
Gasket Mount (Poron & Silicone)

The undisputed standard of modern enthusiast keyboards. The plate features extended mounting tabs cushioned by high-density Poron foam strips or molded silicone socks.

  • •
    Decoupled Acoustics: Prevents metal-on-metal vibration from traveling from the switches into the rigid aluminum case.
  • •
    Cushioned Bottom-Out: Soft vertical compression eliminates harsh jarred stops on fingers during rapid typing.
Alternative Mounting Styles
  • •
    Gummy O-Ring Mount (Bakeneko / F1-8X): A large continuous silicone O-ring stretches around the plate/PCB sandwich and friction-fits into the case posts. Delivers ultra-uniform perimeter flex and clean acoustic resonance.
  • •
    Top Mount: The plate is screwed directly into the upper case rim. Highly consistent stiffness, crisp acoustic clack, and zero mushiness.
  • •
    Tray Mount: The PCB screws onto standoffs in the bottom case (common in budget 60% cases). Prone to stiff center spots and uneven acoustic reverb.
Studio Insight

In gasket builds like the Leobog Hi75C Pro and Weikav WK75, tuned Poron foam strips allow 1.5mm - 2.5mm of vertical displacement, delivering that sought-after bouncy typing sensation.

LAYER 06 • SOUND SCULPTING

06. Acoustic Dampeners & Acoustic Modding

Sculpting the frequency curve by eliminating hollow case reverberation and accentuating rich bass tones.

Poron Plate Foam IXPE Switch Pad PET Sheet Tempest Tape Mod
Precision Foam Layers
  • •
    Poron Plate Foam (3.0mm - 3.5mm): Sandwiched between the Plate and PCB. Fills empty air cavities, silencing high-frequency resonance and pinging.
  • •
    IXPE Switch Pad (0.5mm): Placed on top of the PCB surface directly under each switch. Shifts acoustic frequencies upward into the marbly, snappy, raindrop-like pop.
  • •
    PET Acoustic Film (0.2mm): Ultra-thin reflective polymer sheet placed below IXPE pad to focus sound waves forward toward the user.
  • •
    Case Dampener (EPDM / Poron / Silicone): Fills the bottom aluminum chamber to prevent hollow box echo.
The Legendary Tempest Tape Mod

Originating in custom modding circles, applying 2 to 3 layers of non-conductive painter's masking tape directly to the back of the PCB acts as an acoustic low-pass filter.

  • •
    Acoustic Low-Pass Filter: Tape absorbs high-frequency vibrations while reflecting low-frequency bass waves back upward through the switches.
  • •
    Sound Transformation: Turns thin, hollow clacks into thick, deep, concentrated thocks on almost any keyboard board.
Studio Philosophy

Over-foaming can choke a keyboard's acoustic character into a generic muted sound. At ThocKeebs, we tune foam density specifically to match your switch housing and plate material.

LAYER 07 • ELECTRICAL BRAIN

07. Printed Circuit Board (PCB) & Firmware

The neural pathway that captures keystrokes, controls RGB illumination, and runs WebHID real-time configurators.

5-Pin Hot-Swap South-Facing LEDs Per-Key Flex Cuts WebHID / WebUSB
Hardware Architecture
  • •
    Kailh Hot-Swap Sockets: Solderless switch sockets rated for 100+ swaps. Lets you swap switches in seconds without touching a soldering iron.
  • •
    South-Facing Orientation: LEDs are located on the bottom of the switch socket, preventing the top of the switch housing from colliding with Cherry profile keycap inner walls (No North-Facing Interference).
  • •
    Per-Key Laser Flex Cuts: Laser cutaways around individual switch sockets that allow each key to flex independently under finger strike.
WebHID & WebUSB Firmware Configurator

Modern custom mechanical keyboards eliminate the need for bloated background desktop apps. Thanks to WebHID and WebUSB API integration, configure keys directly inside your browser.

  • •
    Official Web Drivers: Compatible with QMK/VIA and ThocKeebs-partnered WebUSB drivers (Leobog Hi75C Pro & Weikav WK75). Remap layers, set macros, and calibrate knob encoders in real time.
  • •
    Onboard EEPROM Memory: Keybindings are saved directly to the keyboard's internal flash MCU and persist across any PC, Mac, or Linux machine.
PCB Protection

Always support hot-swap sockets with your finger on the back of the PCB when inserting new switches to avoid pushing the solder pad off the board trace!

LAYER 08 • FOUNDATION & MASS

08. CNC Aluminum Case & Acoustic Weight

The heavyweight chassis that absorbs typing kinetic energy, eliminates desk hollow, and anchors acoustic resonance.

6063 Aluminum CNC Milled Internal Brass / PVD Weight Weight: 1.6kg - 3.2kg
Chassis Engineering & Finishes

High-end keyboards are carved from solid billets of aerospace-grade 6063 aluminum using 5-axis CNC milling machines, resulting in seamless, zero-creak tolerances.

  • •
    Anodized 150-Grit Bead Blast: Velvety smooth metallic touch with scratch-resistant electrochemical color oxidation.
  • •
    Electrophoresis (E-Coat): Flawless, ultra-durable porcelain-like smooth finish popular in retro chalk and cream white custom builds.
  • •
    Chamfered Bezels: Precision angled edge cuts that reflect light and elevate workspace desk aesthetic.
The Physics of Mass: Acoustic Weights

Why do custom keyboards weigh 2kg or more? Mass acts as an acoustic anchor.

  • •
    Inertial Dampening: A heavy brass, copper, or PVD mirror stainless steel internal weight bar absorbs vibrations before they can bounce off your desk surface.
  • •
    Acoustic Chamber Geometry: Tuned hollow proportions inside the bottom case eliminate flutter echoes and standing sound waves.
  • •
    Desk Stability: Non-slip molded silicone feet ensure the keyboard never creeps or rattles even under aggressive competitive gaming keystrokes.
The Build Feel

When you tap a key on a dense 2.5kg custom keyboard, you don't hear plastic shaking on a desk—you feel an authoritative, deep acoustic thump grounded into solid metal.
